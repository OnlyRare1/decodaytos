# DECODAY PRIVACY & POLICY

Last Updated: October 2026
1. Discord Account Identifiers Order Routing

What Discord Data We Collect

When you use /purchase or /retry, the bot records your Discord User ID, username, display name, order ID, selected items/quantities, and lifetime preferred display language in our local encrypted order database so your gift links and support tickets are routed to you.

2. Cryptocurrency Verification On-Chain Only

Blockchain Payment Records

We verify incoming payments across supported networks (LTC, BTC, ETH ERC20, ETH BSC BEP20, SOL, USDT BEP20, and GRAM TON) using public blockchain explorers. Only the public transaction hash (TXID), quoted amount, and timestamp are stored to prevent double-claiming. We never have access to your private keys or seed phrases.

3. Optional Via-Login Fulfillment Auto-Logout

Via-Login Session Security

If you explicitly choose the optional Via Login mode for Random Decos, the credentials and 1x backup code you submit are used strictly to log into the isolated secondary instance, complete your exact remaining item count, and automatically execute the full Discord account logout step immediately upon order completion.

4. Claim Recordings & Support Private Verification

Screen Recordings & Ticket Logs

Screen recordings submitted for claim verification or wrong-decoration replacement requests are reviewed exclusively by authorized DecoDay staff to validate your replacement and are never sold, shared, or published externally.

5. Language & Translation Memory User Preference

Persistent Language Preference

When you select a language via the Change Language button, your chosen language code is associated with your Discord User ID so future embeds and popups automatically appear in your preferred language. You can change or reset it to English at any time.

6. Zero Third-Party Selling Strict Confidentiality

Data Sharing & Retention

We never sell, rent, or trade customer data to third parties. Order records and gift delivery logs are retained solely for order recovery (/retry), audit accuracy, and customer support.
